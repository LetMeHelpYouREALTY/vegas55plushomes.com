## 📊 How to Use Google Trends

### 1. Monitor General Trends

**Weekly Task:** Check Trending Now for:
- Real estate trends in Las Vegas
- Retirement living trends
- Active adult community interest
- Nevada relocation searches

**Monthly Task:** Use Explore tool (empty search) with filters:
- **Location:** United States → Nevada → Las Vegas
- **Category:** Real Estate / Travel / Lifestyle
- **Time Range:** Past 12 months

### 2. Track Your Specific Terms

**Monthly Analysis:**
1. Visit [Google Trends Explore](https://trends.google.com/trends/explore)
2. Add 5 key terms: "Las Vegas 55+ communities", "Del Webb Lake Las Vegas", "Sun City Summerlin", "Henderson 55+ homes", "Las Vegas retirement communities"
3. Set filters:
   - **Time:** Past 12 months
   - **Location:** United States (or specific states: CA, NV, AZ, TX)
   - **Category:** Real Estate
4. Analyze:
   - Which terms have rising interest?
   - Which have highest search volume?
   - What are the related topics and queries?

### 3. Seasonal Content Planning

**Key Patterns to Watch:**
- **January-March:** Peak relocation season (retirees planning moves)
- **April-June:** Spring home buying season
- **July-September:** Summer relocation planning
- **October-December:** Year-end relocation planning

**Action:** Create and publish content 1-2 months before seasonal spikes.

## 🔍 Keyword Research Strategy

### Identify Rising Terms

**Weekly Routine:**
1. Use Explore tool with your primary terms
2. Check "Rising" queries (not just "Top")
3. Look for less competitive, emerging terms
4. Focus on terms relevant to your expertise

**Example Research:**
- Search: "Las Vegas 55+ communities"
- Check "Rising" related queries
- Identify: "Las Vegas 55+ gated communities" (rising)
- Action: Enhance your gated communities content

### Analyze Regional Interest

**Monitor by State:**
- **California:** High interest (relocation source)
- **Nevada:** Your local market
- **Arizona:** Similar market, potential relocation
- **Texas:** Growing retirement destination
- **Utah:** Mountain states interest

**Use this data to:**
- Create location-specific content
- Target ads to high-interest states
- Understand relocation patterns
