# SEO Audit Checklist - Based on Google's SEO Starter Guide

## ✅ Already Implemented

### 1. Help Google Find Your Content
- ✅ **Sitemap.xml**: Dynamic sitemap at `/sitemap.xml` with all pages
- ✅ **Robots.txt**: Properly configured at `/robots.txt`
- ✅ **Search Console Ready**: Verification tag placeholder in layout
- ✅ **Canonical URLs**: Set in metadata for all pages

### 2. Organize Your Site
- ✅ **Descriptive URLs**: 
  - `/communities/sun-city-summerlin`
  - `/las-vegas-55-guide/top-10-gated`
  - `/communities/[slug]`
- ✅ **Logical Directory Structure**:
  - `/communities/` - All community pages
  - `/las-vegas-55-guide/` - Guide resources
  - Clean, hierarchical organization
- ✅ **No Duplicate Content**: Unique content per page

### 3. Make Site Interesting & Useful
- ✅ **Well-Organized Text**: Proper headings (H1, H2, H3), paragraphs, sections
- ✅ **Unique Content**: 1500+ words per page, original content
- ✅ **Up-to-Date**: Recent dates, current information
- ✅ **Helpful & Reliable**: Expert sources, detailed information
- ✅ **People-First**: Content written for users, not just search engines

### 4. Link to Relevant Resources
- ✅ **Good Link Text**: Descriptive anchor text (e.g., "Sun City Summerlin", "Las Vegas 55+ communities")
- ✅ **Internal Links**: Strategic linking between related pages
- ✅ **External Links**: When appropriate (e.g., community builders)

### 5. Title Links & Snippets
- ✅ **Unique Titles**: Each page has unique, descriptive title
- ✅ **Clear & Concise**: Titles accurately describe page content
- ✅ **Meta Descriptions**: Unique descriptions on all pages
- ✅ **Descriptive**: Includes location, business name, key terms

### 6. Technical SEO
- ✅ **Proper HTML Structure**: Semantic HTML, proper heading hierarchy
- ✅ **Mobile Responsive**: All pages mobile-friendly
- ✅ **Fast Loading**: Optimized with Next.js, lazy loading
- ✅ **HTTPS**: Secure connection via Vercel
